# Breed photos

These photos are **not** covered by the Breed Finder repository's copyright. Each one belongs to its photographer and comes from [Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org).

[credits.json](credits.json) lists, for every file:
- the photographer (`author`);
- the license (`license`, with a link in `licenseURL`);
- the photo's Commons page (`sourceURL`), which has the full license terms.

The licenses are public domain, CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA and "copyrighted free use".

Breed Finder resized the photos (longest side 800 px). Under each original license, these resized versions are available on the same terms as the original. CC BY-SA photos stay CC BY-SA.

The app shows each photo's credit on its breed's page and lists them all under Support → Photo credits. A few credits are corrected by hand from the Commons page in `CREDIT_OVERRIDES`, in [tools/build_breed_content.py](../../tools/build_breed_content.py).
