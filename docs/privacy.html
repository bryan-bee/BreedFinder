Breed Finder Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Breed Finder analyzes your dog on your iPhone, and your photos and collection stay there. The app sends anonymous usage stats, which you can turn off. We receive anything else only if you choose to send us a problem report.

The camera

Breed Finder uses your iPhone's camera only when you tap Scan a Dog, to record a 5-second video. It records video only, with no sound, and never uses the microphone. If you turn on the flash, it lights the dog only while recording.

The app pulls 5 still frames out of the video and analyzes them on your iPhone to guess the dog's breed. The video file is deleted from your phone as soon as the scan finishes. Nothing is saved to your photo library.

Photos you choose

If you tap Choose Photos, the iPhone's own photo picker opens. Breed Finder only receives the photos you select (up to 5), not the rest of your library, and analyzes them on your iPhone the same way. The copies it keeps are re-saved images without the location or other details stored in the original photo files.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your Breeds collection. For each scan that finds a dog, the app saves the breed it counts as, the date, the app's guess and how sure it was, and a small photo of your dog cropped from the scan. You can remove a scan in the Breeds tab (touch and hold its photo), or on a result by tapping Change, then Don't count this scan.
  • Your last 10 scans. The frames or photos and the results of your 10 most recent scans are kept in the app's temporary storage so you can attach one to a problem report. Older ones are deleted automatically as you scan, and iOS may clear them when your iPhone needs space.
  • Two settings: how you like the Breeds list grouped, and whether usage stats are on.

These photos and records are never sent anywhere. The usage stats below include a few counts about them, such as how many breeds you've collected, but never the photos. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Anonymous usage stats

To learn how well Breed Finder works on real dogs, the app sends anonymous usage stats to TelemetryDeck, an analytics service based in Germany. You can turn them off at any time in the app under Support → Share anonymous usage stats. While they're off, nothing is sent.

What's sent:

  • when you open the app, to count how often it's used;
  • when a scan finishes or finds no dog: whether it was a video or photos, how many frames had a dog in them, how long the analysis took on your iPhone, which breed model was used, and how many breeds you've collected;
  • the breed the app guessed and how sure it was, your answer to Is it a …? on the result, and the breed you picked if you answered no;
  • if a scan fails, which step failed and its error code;
  • whether you recorded with the camera zoomed out, zoomed in or with the flash on;
  • when you send a problem report: the kind of problem, whether photos were attached, whether you allowed them to be used for training, and how you sent it, but never what you wrote;
  • details TelemetryDeck adds to each of these: your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, language, region, time zone, screen size, display and accessibility settings (such as dark mode, text size and Reduce Motion), and counts such as how many days you've used the app.

What's never sent: your photos or videos, anything you type, your name, email address or location. TelemetryDeck doesn't store IP addresses. To count how many people use the app, it uses the identifier iOS gives this app on your iPhone. The app scrambles that identifier with a one-way hash before sending it, and TelemetryDeck hashes it again on its servers, so we see "an install", never who you are.

We look at the stats only as totals and averages, such as how many scans found a dog and how often the top guess was right, to fix problems and improve the breed model. They aren't used for ads or tracking, aren't combined with data from other companies, and aren't sold. We collect them based on our legitimate interest in making the app work well (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)); turning them off is how you object. TelemetryDeck processes them for us under its privacy policy.

Problem reports

If you tap Report a Problem and send the email it prepares, we receive what you choose to send, through your own email app:

  • your email address, and your name if your email app includes it;
  • what you wrote, the kind of problem, and the breed you said the dog is;
  • if you leave them attached, the frames or photos of the scan you picked, and what the app guessed for it;
  • your app version, breed model version, iOS version and iPhone model.

You see the whole email, including any photos, before it's sent, and you can edit or cancel it. We use reports to reply to you and to fix problems. We use photos from a report to train Breed Finder's breed recognition only if you turned on Breed Finder may use these photos to improve breed recognition in that report. We don't sell or share reports. Ask us and we'll delete a report you sent.

Crash reports

If you've turned on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may send us anonymous crash logs for Breed Finder. Apple's privacy policy covers that process.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads and no tracking.
  • No third-party code that collects data, other than TelemetryDeck's for the anonymous usage stats.
  • Apart from those stats, the app doesn't connect to the internet. Links you tap, like photo credits, open in your browser, and problem reports go through your email app.

Breed photos

The breed reference photos are included with the app when you download it. They come from Wikimedia Commons, and the app credits each photo's author and license.

Children

Breed Finder isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from them. If a child sends us a problem report, we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
