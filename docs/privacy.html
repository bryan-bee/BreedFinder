Breed Finder Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026

Breed Finder does not collect, store, or share any of your data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

The camera

Breed Finder uses your iPhone's camera only when you tap Scan a Dog, to record a 5-second video. It records video only, with no sound, and never uses the microphone.

The app pulls 5 still frames out of the video and analyzes them on your iPhone to guess the dog's breed. The video file is deleted from your phone as soon as the scan finishes. The frames shown on the result screen are held only in the app's memory and are cleared when you go back to the home screen or close the app. Nothing is saved to your photo library.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, or tracking.
  • No third-party code that collects data.
  • The app doesn't connect to the internet, so your videos and frames never leave your iPhone.

Breed photos

The breed reference photos in the app are included with it when you download it. They come from Wikimedia Commons, and the app credits each photo's author and license.

Children

Breed Finder collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
