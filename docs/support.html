Breed Finder Support

Answers to common questions are below. If something isn't covered, get in touch.

Email: breedfinder123@gmail.com

In the app: open the Support tab and tap Report a Problem. You can attach one of your recent scans, and you'll see the whole email before it's sent.

How does Breed Finder work?

Tap Scan a Dog and record 5 seconds of video, or tap Choose Photos and pick up to 5 photos. Breed Finder takes 5 frames from the video, finds the dog in each one, and a breed-recognition model on your iPhone guesses the breed. You get the top 3 guesses, how sure the app is about each, and facts about the breed. Nothing is uploaded.

How accurate is it?

On 9,629 test photos, the right breed was the app's first guess 92% of the time and in its top 3 98% of the time. Real scans are harder, with blur, poor light and mixed breeds, which is why the app asks Is it a …? after each scan. The percentages are calibrated: when the app says 90%, it's right about 9 times out of 10.

It says "No dog found"

  • Scan in good light, with the dog filling more of the picture.
  • Keep the dog in view for the whole 5 seconds: at least 2 of the 5 frames need to show it.
  • If the dog is too close to fit, tap 0.5× to zoom out (on iPhones with an ultra-wide camera).
  • Or use Choose Photos with a clear photo of the dog.

The result shows which frames had a dog in them, so you can see what went wrong.

It guessed the wrong breed

Under the cards, answer No to Is it a …? and pick the right breed: the app's other two guesses come first, or tap Another breed… for the full list. Your Breeds collection then counts the scan as the breed you picked. Your answers also help us measure how accurate the app is.

To help improve the breed model, tap Send this correction to help improve Breed Finder on the result. You choose whether the photos may be used for training.

My dog is a mixed breed

Breed Finder knows 176 breeds, including popular doodles and other designer crossbreeds. For other mixes, the top 3 often shows the parent breeds. It's a best guess from how the dog looks, not a DNA test.

My dog's breed isn't in the app

Some breeds don't have enough freely licensed photos to learn from yet, so the app names the closest breed it knows. Let us know which breed through Report a Problem, and we'll try to add it.

How do I remove a scan from my Breeds?

On the result, tap Change, then Don't count this scan. Later on, open the breed in the Breeds tab, touch and hold the scan's photo, and choose Remove from Breeds.

The camera doesn't open

Breed Finder needs permission to use the camera. Open the iPhone's Settings, tap Breed Finder, and turn on Camera.

Are my photos or videos uploaded?

No. Scans are analyzed on your iPhone, and your photos and collection stay there. The app sends anonymous usage stats, such as how many scans find a dog and how often the top guess is right, which you can turn off under Support → Share anonymous usage stats. The privacy policy has the details.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone with iOS 17 or later.

Where do the breed photos come from?

From Wikimedia Commons, used under each photo's license. The photographer and license are credited under every breed's profile, and all together under Support → Photo credits.
